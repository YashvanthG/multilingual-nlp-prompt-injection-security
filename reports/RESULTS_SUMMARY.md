## C. Synthetic-data results — what they do and do not show
The agent-style data is generated by `data/build_agent_style.py` (authored by this project, same author as the regression scenarios). Train and held-out use
disjoint phrase pools; there are no exact overlaps and no near-duplicates (max TF-IDF cosine between held-out and agent-style training rows: none ≥ 0.80, median 0.26).
Its held-out attacks are easy (0% missed), so B/C show reduced benign false escalation, not attack-detection strength. The held-out set has 299 English and **4 Spanish**
rows: this is insufficient to claim any multilingual robustness.
